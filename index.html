Rack & Log

Your Mon–Fri plan. Log each set and rate how it felt, then tap Finish & save workout. The app suggests your next weights and tracks your progress in the Log tab.

How suggestions work
  • Hit the top of the rep range on every set and rate them Easy or Good: the weight goes up one jump next time.
  • Every set Easy at the top of the range: it goes up two jumps.
  • Anything rated Hard, or below the top of the range: stay at that weight and add reps.
  • Missed reps two sessions running: drop one jump and build back up.
  • Set the weight jump on each exercise to match your gym's dumbbells and machines.
Weaker leg rules
  • Weaker leg goes first on every single-leg exercise.
  • The stronger leg matches the weaker leg's reps, no more.
  • One extra set for the weaker leg on single-leg leg extension and single-leg leg press.
  • Aim for the weaker leg within 10% of the stronger leg. The app compares them from your logged sets.
Deload and knee safety
  • Every 6th week: 2 sets per exercise at about 60% of your usual weight. Rate those sets honestly but ignore the suggestions that week.
  • Stop and ease off for a few days if you get sharp knee pain, swelling the next morning, or the knee feels unstable. See your physio if it keeps happening.
